The Floor is Lava! (Saturday, 12 September 2026)Click to collapse

Organiser:
Justin Atkinson

We're helping the Greater Wellington Backcountry Network to install track boardwalks in the Tararuas! This is an overnight trip with camping in the bush ⛺

We'll rendezvous with helicopters carrying supplies, and then help carry the rafts to their drop-off points.

Participants need to be experienced with steep Tararua tramping conditions and capable of heavy lifting.

NOTE: This trip is medium to medium-hard difficulty. The walk involves 5km walk and several hundred metres of ascent.

Dobson Loop track info

Day plan for Sat 12 Sep:

VUWTC people meet at Kelburn campus at 6:45am. We'll then drive 45min to the track start at Kaitoke, where GWBN will meet us at 8am.

1 person will fly up to Dobson's Track, the rest will walk up (including me). The walk involves a mix of steep and flat sections of track. We will drop our packs and tools at the helicopter rendezvous points, and carry boardwalks for short periods. Overall walking time will be at least 2-3 hours, I think maximum time is 5-6 hours.

Gear:

Please bring day tramping gear. Key things are a tramping pack, warm clothing, a windproof raincoat, tramping boots, a bowl/cutlery, a headtorch, a big water bottle, and a first aid kit/personal medication.

Full gear list here.

Food:

Please bring lunch, dinner, plenty of snacks, and lots of water

Cost:

- $7.50 fuel per person

Weekend Trip to Jumbo Hut (via Mt Holdsworth) (Saturday, 12 - Sunday, 13 September 2026)Click to collapse

Organiser:
Hazel Murray

This overnight tramp is for anyone wishing to complete the Jumbo Circuit and stay at an awesome alpine hut.

The days walking on this trip will be 6-8 hours, and anyone signing up should have a medium level of tramping experience. 

 

On Saturday: We will meet at Hunter Carpark at 7.45 to leave at 8, so that we can make the most of the daylight on our walk (6hrs).

We will then tramp along the valley to Atiwhakatu hut and then straight up to Jumbo, to stay the night. 

I will organise dinner to cook as a group, it will be traditional, delectable and celebratory. It will be nachos. 

On Sunday: Fuelled or propelled by beans we will walk up to the Summit of Jumbo and then along the ridge to Holdsworth

before descending past Powell Hut down many, many stairs and the lovely Gentle Annie track, back to where we started (8hrs). 

 

Cost: Hut tickets at $25 each, petrol for carpooling and $6 per person for dinner will all be included in the trip cost. 

Pease bring: Breakfast and lunch for two days, a functioning headtorch with spare batteries, sturdy boots and all your other tramping essentials, 

see our quick gear list here: https://www.vuwtc.org.nz/resources/gear-list

Peer Instructed Glacier Course (Friday, 18 - Sunday, 20 September 2026)Click to expand

Organisers:
Kathleen Griffin
Jack Goodeve
Anton Afanassiev

This course will be run by two club members, Kathleen & Anton. We are not professional instructors, but I'd like to think we're alright teachers & we have experience running similar courses for other clubs. 

We will spend the weekend at Ruapehu, learning the basics of glacier travel & crevasse rescue including:

  • How to set up & travel as a rope team
  • How to probe out a campsite
  • How to make safe decisions on glaciers
  • How to build snow anchors
  • How to do a basic crevasse rescue

This is just an introductory course that will give you the skills to travel across mild terrain in good conditions. There is still a lot more than we can fit in one weekend, so participants are encouraged to continue practicing & learning after the course. 

 

As there are only 8 learning spots, this trip will not be first come first served. Participants will be chosen based on:

  • Proven commitment to the club
  • Leadership or instructing experience
  • Stoke - keen people are more likely to use these skills

This criteria is based on the idea that the participants will be able to pass this knowledge on to other club members in a future course of similar format. 
So if you do miss out on this one, hopefully there will be more opportunites coming soon.

Other than that, the minimum requirements are:

  • You must have done snowcraft
  • You must attend a pre-trip weeknight training session (https://www.vuwtc.org.nz/Event/14522 and/or https://www.vuwtc.org.nz/Event/14523)
  • You may also be required to buy a small amount of personal gear, but we will keep this as minimal as possible. 

 

Regarding Sign-ups: Sign-ups will close on the 30th of Aug and we will let aim let people know who is coming by then.

Birdwood Reserve Working Bee - Conservation (Sunday, 20 September 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Hazel Murray

 

Organiser: Hazel Murray (email)

An ongoing collaboration with Karori Kaitiaki at Birdwood Reserve. We will be helping to restore a steep Gully of native bush in the reserve, and clearing Kaiwharawhara stream of Tradescandia. This is an important site for Karori Kaitiaki, as it's the home of the future Birdwood Reserve track. It's a super fun way to do some practical work outside that creates meaningful and visible change within the native ecology of our city. Come along and be part of the action!

Please bring gardening gloves, a water bottle and jacket, snacks and if possible a spade, shovel or rake. 

Hope to see you there!

Wednesday Climb Night @ Faultline (Wednesday, 16 September 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Sonia Flux

Welcome to Wednesday Climbing Nights!

Want to try indoor climbing for the first time, or meet some like-minded climbers? Come along to our weekly climb nights! We climb at Hangdog (Bouldering & Top roping) in Lower Hutt, and Faultline (Bouldering) on Willis St.

This week, we'll be climbing at Faultline, which is a bouldering gym. This means no ropes/harnesses and no belaying. There are grades for every level of experience, so no matter how much climbing experience you have you’ll have plenty to get stuck into!

IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST TIME CLIMBING AT FAULTLINE, SCROLL TO THE BOTTOM OF THIS PAGE (https://www.wellingtonclimbing.co.nz/first_visit_fl/) AND SIGN THE ONLINE WAIVER BEFORE WE LEAVE FOR THE GYM!

This week: Faultline

Schedule:

  • Meet outside the VUWSA building at 5:00pm to walk down together or outside Faultline at 5:20 p.m.
  • CLIMB!
  • You can leave whenever you like (we usually finish up around 7:30-8:00 p.m) and return home independently.

Entry costs: $15 + $3 for shoes

This is not paid for through the club—there is no piking fee if you pull out. Pay at Faultline when we arrive.

To get the VUWTC entry price, just let the front desk know you are with VUWTC. Our discounted price is only from 5:30 pm onwards on Wednesdays.

See you there :)

Kapakapanui Overnighter (Saturday, 5 - Sunday, 6 September 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
E Kelly

An overnighter to Kapakapanui hut, featuring some stunning goblin forest bush!

Doc track information

Kapakapanui hut is a $15 ticket (no booking required) and sleeps 6 people. Trip is currently limited to one car (5 people) but if people are super keen and willing to sleep under flys i will consider opening it up to more people. 

This route requires multiple small river crossings at the very beginning, so you will be getting wet boots (unless you bring a dedicated second pair to wear for the beginning). Heavy rain has the potential to make the river crossings an issue, so we will be careful and decide whether this trip should go ahead based on the weather. If the river is too high on the return trip, we will wait at the hut (bring extra food) until the water goes down.

This is a short, relatively easy trip, but it requires a steep climb, so you will need some level of fitness for a steep muddy climb. We will take it super easy and have lots of breaks.

 It is about a 1-hour drive to the road-end; we will leave on Saturday morning and aim to return to campus by mid afternoon on sunday.

Please bring lunch, dinner, breakfast and snacks!!

Here is a link to the gear list - please bring plenty of warm clothing!! - if you need to borrow gear let me know.

Gear list

Walking times are:
Road end -> Kapakapanui hut (3-4hrs)
Kapakapanui summit return trip (optional) (2 hrs); we will aim to do this on Saturday afternoon.

Outdoor Bouldering Series @ Baring Head (Saturday, 12 September 2026)Click to expand

Organisers:
Sonia Flux
Lucy Thorburn

Kia ora koutou!

Ever wanted to escape Faultline and breathe some fresh air while climbing? Ever wanted to learn a few tips and tricks on how to climb rocks? Nau mai haere mai ki ētahi haerenga. 

This trip is one of the missions out to the Wairarapa Coast. Here, you will learn how to spot someone while climbing, use bouldering mats, and progress your climbing skills. If you have gone to a few climb nights, but haven't climbed outdoors yet, this trip is for you. Heoi ano, if you have experience outdoor climbing, please sign up as well as it would be great to have some people with experience.

The number of people we can take on the trip will depend on how many cars sign up.

 

What do you need to know?

Where will we be going? Baring Head, a famous Welly bouldering spot, about a 50 minute drive from Vic Uni.

 

What's the plan? 

- Meet at 8:45am on Saturday the 12th of September at Hunter Carpark

- Drive to Baring Head (50 mins)

- Walk to the climbing spots (50 mins)

- CLIMB! (and enjoy the view)

- Leave the climbing spots by 3pm to get back to uni by 5pm.

 

What should I bring? 

- Walking shoes

- Climbing shoes and chalk

- Warm layers (including raincoat - it gets very windy)

- Kai for snacks and lunch

- At least 1.5 litres of water

- Torch (just in case)

 

How experienced should I be to go? Baring Head has lots of different grades, but in general this trip will have more variety of climbs for people that can climb 3-4+ at Faultline. Even if you're a bit below that feel free to come along and give it a go. The coastline is beautiful as well if you want to sit and watch the sea + take lots of breaks. 

 

Here is a link to the climbs at Baring Head! https://climbnz.org.nz/nz/ni/wellington/baring-head
