Wednesday Climb Night @ Hangdog (Wednesday, 30 September 2026)Click to collapse

Organiser:
Sonia Flux

Welcome to Wednesday Climbing Nights!

Want to try indoor climbing for the first time, or meet some like-minded climbers? Come along to our weekly climb nights! We climb at Hangdog (Bouldering & Top-roping) in Lower Hutt, and Faultline (Bouldering) on Willis St. 

This week, we'll be climbing at Hangdog, which is a top-roping and bouldering gym. This means some climbs will require ropes, harnesses & belaying. There are grades for every level of experience, so no matter how much climbing experience you have you’ll have plenty to get stuck into! 

 

This week: Hangdog (bring cars!)

Schedule:

  • Sign up! (Even if you are meeting us there - they need to know numbers ahead of time)

  • Meet at Hunter carpark at 5 pm, leave for the gym at 5:10 pm on the dot.

  • CLIMB!

  • You can leave whenever you like - but drivers need to have the same number of people in their cars that they brought out. (Drivers - make sure the people you drove out know when you want to leave!)

  • PAY YOUR DRIVERS 

Costs: $15 entry + $3 for shoes + $3 for your driver

This is not paid for through the club so there is no piking fee if you pull out. Pay at the gym when we arrive/to your driver directly.

To get the VUWTC entry price, just let the front desk know you are with VUWTC. Our discounted price is only from 5:30 pm onwards on Wednesdays.

See you there :)

Organiser:
Lucy Thorburn

Bushcraft 2 - An incredible journey for you to develop skills in navigation, river crossing, devouring food in the bush aka foraging, risk management and first aid! This trip is open to all who have a reasonable level of tramping experience and who are ready to learn what to do when things might not go to plan...

For Bushcraft 2, we will be heading to Tutuwai Hut on Friday the 2nd of October at 2pm, meeting at the Hunter Carpark at 1:30 pm. We will aim to be back by around 5pm on the Sunday. 

We will be fly camping at Tutuwai Hut, and the gear list is attached below. If you have your own compass and tararua map (Topo50), please bring it.

https://www.vuwtc.org.nz/resources/gear-list

If you want to learn more about Navigation, sign up to the nav course as well on the 15th of September.

Bring your family, friends, neighbours and anyone along. Snacks encouraged!

Snowcraft 2 (Friday, 25 - Sunday, 27 September 2026)Click to expand

Organisers:
Max Honiss
Jack Goodeve
Lucy Thorburn

The Second Snowcaft

 

 

We run two snowcrafts each year, this is our second one.

Dates: 25th - 27th of Sep

On Friday evening we will meet at the gear shed from 4pm to sus ourselves out, then start driving north by 4:30pm. Bulls is about halfway & has a bangin' curry spot so maybe pop in for dinner on the way, or elsewhere (up to your driver really). We should get to Ruapehu before midnight, then we have to walk about 20min from the carpark to the lodge. 

Saturday morning we will rise at a reasonable hour, have breakfast, and sort the day's gameplan. We will split into groups, and spend the day on the mountain learning snow skills, & checking out some cool scenery. Then we'll head back down to the lodge for a mean feed & a bit of socialising. 

Sunday morning we have to tidy up the lodge but otherwise we can decide what we want to do depending on conditions and how people are feeling. There's potential for more snow stuff, or maybe a trip to the hotpools or other locations on the way home. We will likely be back in Wellington late on Sunday.

What do I need for snowcraft?

A comprehensive list will be sent out to those attending, but here's a rough outline of the kind of gear you need:

  • Waterproof jacket, pants & gloves

  • Boots that are compatible with crampons - this is very important! Bring your boots to a meeting if you're not sure. 

  • Sleeping bag

  • Warm clothes - thermals, fleece etc

  • Headtorch

  • Snow goggles or decent sunglasses

VUWTC will supply helmets, ice axes, and crampons

 

How do I sign up?

Sign up using the sign up form below. Sign ups will open on the 17th of August and close on the 23rd


Due to the popularity of this trip it will not be first in first served - priority will be given to members that are currently active in the club (coming to meetings/going on trips) & those who signed up for Snowcraft one, but where not selected.
We are allowed 31 people in the lodge so that will be the trip capacity

 

Snowcraft 1: $150 for those going on the course, and $135 for those leading groups. (These were the costs for snowcraft 1, they may be subject ot change).

  • The fees for staying 2 night in the TTC lodge
  • 2 breakfasts, 2 lunches, and 1 dinner/dessert
  • Your share of petrol (it's a long drive!)
