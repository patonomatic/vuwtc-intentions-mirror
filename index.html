Bush Rave - Spooky Edition (Saturday, 31 October - Sunday, 1 November 2026)Click to expand

Organisers:
Grace Rotherham
Mia Baigent

Join us for the biggest rave of the year! 

We will have a lineup of DJs, some incredible beats, and your fantastic dance moves! All in the bush! This is an overnight event, but no tramping is involved; we will drive to a campsite (location to be revealed), set up tents, and then party! We will camp overnight in tents/flys. 

Theme

This year's theme is SPOOKY FOREST. Come dressed in your spookiest outfit!

When?

Meet at 2 pm on Saturday the 31st of October at the Hunter Carpark outside the gear shed. Everyone must be there by 2:30pm!

On Sunday the 1st of November, the bus will pick us up at 11am and have us back at uni around 12pm.

Where?

The Bush Rave will be somewhere in the bush, within an hour's drive of Welly! The location will be revealed via email to everyone with a ticket the day before. 

Transport

We are hiring a bus to transport the majority of people, but we will also need some drivers. If you can bring a car, please let us know; you will be reimbursed for fuel.

 

What you need to bring:

  • Your Fabulous Costume!

  • Your dinner, breakfast, and plenty of snacks! 

  • Your beverage of choice - no glass please

  • Tent (Bring your own or find a spot under one of the club's flies)

  • Pack + Pack liner/drybags

  • Raincoat

  • Headtorch + spare batteries

  • Boots + socks

  • Shorts + teeshirt not cotton

  • Fleece/wool jumper not cotton

  • Thermal top and bottom

  • Warm hat, gloves and neck gaiter

  • Sunhat + sunscreen

  • 1L+ water bottle

  • Personal first aid kit + personal medication

  • Toilet paper + hand sanitizer

  • Sleeping bag

  •  Sleeping mat

  • Toothbrush + toiletries

  •  Bowl, mug and cutlery

It will get cold! Please bring plenty of warm clothes!

If you need to borrow any gear, eg sleeping bag/sleeping mat, please get in touch with the gear custodians: https://www.instagram.com/vuwtc_gear/ 

You must be a club member to borrow gear. Gear can be collected on Saturday before we get on the bus, please come at least 15mins early (1:45 pm) to get this sorted.

biv-bagging feat. the broken axe pinnacles (Tramps Week) (Wednesday, 4 - Friday, 6 November 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Frank Monastra Woods

POSTPONED - dates now confirmed!

This will be a great mission to explore some tops on the eastern tararuas and bag 2 of the tararua's 6 (official) bivvies. We will also go check out the broken axe pinnacles which should be quite cool.

This is a decent few days walking with a bit of weather-dependent off-track tops travel, so strong fitness and experience is required.

Day 1: Holdsworth carpark to mid king biv via atiwhakatu hut

Day 2: mid king biv to mcgregor biv via broken axe pinnacles

Day 3: mcgregor biv to holdworth carpark via jumbo

As per usual with the Tararuas, the weather is super unpredictable, so if we don't deem it wise to head up onto the tops, we will head to mid king biv via the barton track to stay below the bushline and then head out the same way as a 2-day trip.

Gear needed:

Standard tramping stuff

Tents - we will be borrowing 1 or 2 from the club just in case the biv/s are full!

Don't forget your headtorch! We may be walking in the dark a bit.

Gaiters wouldn't go amiss for this trip - lots of leatherwood and spaniards around mid king!

Food: I will sus 2x dinners, please bring 2x breakfasts and 3x lunches, and snacks!

Cost: fees for this trip go towards dinner and petrol.

Organiser:
Jack Goodeve

You are cordially invited to the annual summit luncheon between the good folks of VUWTC and AUTC.

Snowcraft experience required. Cricket, sock wrestling, and table-traversing experience strongly preferred.

Staying at the TTC lodge, room for 16 of us to go.

 

We will be staying in the TTC Lodge with members from the AUTC (Auckland University Tramping Club). Many shenanigans will be undertaken, mulled wine will be drunk, cricket will be played on the summit plateu, and we will wrestle over socks. Perhaps we may sing songs too.

 

Gear required is the same as was used on Snowcraft. So please ensure you have all the right layers and crampon compatible boots. 

Food: We will do a group meal on the Saturday night that will be shared with AUTC. That is the only shared meal. The rest will be self catered. 2x Lunches, 2x Breakfasts, and plenty of snacks!

Birds, Bingo and Butcher Saddle (Tramps Week) (Saturday, 17 - Monday, 19 October 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Micah Langham

CHANGED ROUTE:

DAY 1 - WALK TO WAIOPEHU 

DAY 2 - WALK TO SOUTH OHAU 

DAY 3 - EITHER : walk out via river (if it is low enough to be safe) or walk out via Gable End ridge

 

Kia ora all!

If birds, the bush and bingo are up your alley then this is a tramp for you. Over three days we will walk up the Ōhau river, up and over twin peak, and back down through the bush. Over this time, each of us will have a bingo sheet of native birds to spot – with huge prizes (like bird-sightings) up for grabs!

This trip will run from Saturday the 17th till the Monday the 19th of October, leaving Saturday morning and getting back Monday evening.

I will send out a gear list closer to the time but feel free refer to the website for a general idea (just normal tramping things – plus maybe binoculars and a bird ID book if you’re feeling enthusiastic).

Dinner will be provided (and divvied between packs) but you will need to bring your own breakfast, lunch and snacks.

There is a chance the route will change slightly if it rains a lot (or is forecasted to) and we need to avoid the river.

Numbers are limited by hut capacity, and by drivers (2 people with cars would be great!)

Cheers,
Micah (soon to be bird spotting pro)

 

 

(The cost is for 2 dinners + fuel for driving to and from)
