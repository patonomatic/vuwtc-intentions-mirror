Welcome to Wednesday Climbing Nights!

Want to try indoor climbing for the first time, or meet some like-minded climbers? Come along to our weekly climb nights! We climb at Hangdog (Bouldering & Top roping) in Lower Hutt, and Faultline (Bouldering) on Willis St.

Schedule:

AGM 2026 (Tuesday, 13 October 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Max Honiss

Mark the Changing of the Guard with our Annual General Meeting! Decide who will be on the committee for 2027. The venue is SU228.

 

Nominate new committee members here: 

Below are some brief descriptions of what each role does, so have a read and a think.

President: 

  • Run the meetings or delegate to someone else every other meeting. This includes making the meeting slides.
  • Make sure committee meetings: Ensuring things get done when they need to happen (Freshers, Heels). 
  • First point of contact: Typically you will be the first one to reach out to, whether that be new members or other queries.
  • Be a friendly face: You are the face of the club so use it wisely ;)
  • Co-manage the [email protected] email with the Secretary.

Secretary:

  • Take minutes at committee meetings, special general meetings, and annual general meetings.
  • Manage [email protected], jointly with the president.
  • Manage physical mail - this includes letters, FMC cards, Backcountry magazines, gear donations, and other things. Generally, we don't get too much mail.
  • Once a year, send annual financial statements to the Registrar of Incorporated Societies.
  • Keep club details updated on the Register, and let the Registrar know of any changes to the constitution.
  • Once a year, re-register the club with Federated Mountain Clubs and the university.
  • Once a year, apply for grant funding from the university, with help from the rest of the committee.
  • Book the weekly meeting room and the clubs week stalls or get someone else to.
  • Keep the Register of Members.
  • Hold the club's records, documents, and books except those required for the Treasurer’s function.
  • Attend in-person and online meetings, such as with FMC, Mountain Safety Council, and Zealandia Te Māra a Tāne.

Chief Guide & Assistant Chief Guide:

  • Run trips!! The chief guides are responsible for all-things tramping. Run tons of trips and encourage & help others in the club to run trips too!
  • Organize and lead the main annual trips which are: Snowcraft 1 & 2, Bushcraft and Freshers (shared with the social officers).
  • Plus organizing Midwinter, which involves encouraging people to run larger trips over the uni holidays (and hopefully run a big one yourself!).
  • Act as the main source of information for people in the club about tramping, like giving advice and helping others plan & run trips.
  • Manage the [email protected] email.
  • Quit your day job and spend every weekend tramping!

Treasurer & Co-Treasurer:

  • Deal with payments for trips, including the big ones (Freshers, Bush rave etc)
  • Keep track of the club’s annual budget
  • Help with any payment issues for club members
  • Chase up people who owe money
  • Manage the [email protected] email
  • No treasurer = No more tramps! 

Climbing Officer:

  • Run weekly climb nights
  • Organises Rockcraft 
  • Organise/help others organise outdoor trips 
  • Be fun and welcoming and get people climbing!! 

Social Convenor x 2:

  • Organises all of the party trips: Tramps X3, Freshers (With chief guides), Bush ball, Midwinter Xmas, Bush rave
    • Finding location, organising setup crew, setting theme etc
  • Be a friendly face at these events and others 
  • Option to organise more social trips outside of these

Conservation Officer:

  • Run a bunch of conservation trips!
  • Promote conservation/environment/sustainability causes, like Conservation Week, Bird of the Year, NZ Garden Bird Survey, Earth Day, Plastic Free July etc.
  • Maintain connections with long-standing partners of VUWTC: DOC, Sustainable Coastlines, Zealandia Te Māra a Tāne, uni clubs like VUW SCB and VUW Science Collective.
  • Build new relationships with conservation and community groups.
  • Possibly do 2-3 beach cleanups out at Opau Bay each year (VUWTC looks after that site).
  • Help VUWTC contribute to conservation/environment related legislation if appropriate.
  • Arrange conservation guest speakers if possible!
  • Purchase/look after club conservation gear.
  • Manage the [email protected] email.

Gear Custodian x 2:

  •  Put gear in members’ hands
  • Influence how club gear budget is spent
  • Repair gear and give it new life
  • Preserve club’s historic artifacts
  •  Run working bees to do the heavy lifting
  • Use of the gearshed workshop

Publicity Officer x 2:

  • Manages the social media accounts; the Instagram account (@vuwtc), the Facebook page, and don't forget the TikTok! ;)
  • Manages the vuwtc publicity email [email protected].
  • Ensures the club is engaged on social media, by posting past trips, advertising upcoming trips, sending out reminders for weekly meetings, climbing nights & walkies etc
  • Organises the creation and sale of merch
  • Finds/makes good merch designs (hoodies, shirts, beanies, badges, stickers...), reach out to design companies, gets quotes to ensure we stick to budget, and ensures they get deliver

Hut Maintenance Officer:

  • Gets the club involved in maintenance of huts and tracks
  • Is our representative to the Greater Wellington Backcountry Network (GWBN)
  • Would be great to have a keen person in this role

HEELs Officer:

  • Leads the production of the annual magazine 'HEELS'; 
  • Leads a group in charge of the design & writing of HEELS, 'hassles' people to write trip reports, reach out to book printing publishes, ensures we stick to both the printing guidelines and the budget

Bush Rave - Spooky Edition (Saturday, 10 - Sunday, 11 October 2026)Click to expand

Organisers:
Max Honiss
Grace Rotherham
Mia Baigent

Join us for the biggest rave of the year! 

We will have a lineup of DJs, some incredible beats, and your fantastic dance moves! All in the bush! This is an overnight event, but no tramping is involved; we will drive to a campsite (location to be revealed), set up tents, and then party! We will camp overnight in tents/flys. 

Theme

This year's theme is SPOOKY FOREST. Come dressed in your spookiest outfit!

When?

Meet at 2 pm on Saturday the 10th of October at the Hunter Carpark outside the gear shed. Everyone must be there by 2:30pm!

On Sunday the 11th, the bus will pick us up at 11am and have us back at uni around 12pm.

Where?

The Bush Rave will be somewhere in the bush, within an hour's drive of Welly! The location will be revealed via email to everyone with a ticket the day before. 

Transport

We are hiring a bus to transport the majority of people, but we will also need some drivers. If you can bring a car, please let us know; you will be reimbursed for fuel.

 

What you need to bring:

  • Your Fabulous Costume!

  • Your dinner, breakfast, and plenty of snacks! 

  • Your beverage of choice - no glass please

  • Tent (Bring your own or find a spot under one of the club's flies)

  • Pack + Pack liner/drybags

  • Raincoat

  • Headtorch + spare batteries

  • Boots + socks

  • Shorts + teeshirt not cotton

  • Fleece/wool jumper not cotton

  • Thermal top and bottom

  • Warm hat, gloves and neck gaiter

  • Sunhat + sunscreen

  • 1L+ water bottle

  • Personal first aid kit + personal medication

  • Toilet paper + hand sanitizer

  • Sleeping bag

  •  Sleeping mat

  • Toothbrush + toiletries

  •  Bowl, mug and cutlery

It will get cold! Please bring plenty of warm clothes!

If you need to borrow any gear, eg sleeping bag/sleeping mat, please get in touch with the gear custodians: https://www.instagram.com/vuwtc_gear/ 

You must be a club member to borrow gear. Gear can be collected on Saturday before we get on the bus, please come at least 15mins early (1:45 pm) to get this sorted.

Southern Crossing (Tramps Week) (Friday, 16 - Sunday, 18 October 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Tommy Bollinger

 

This addition to the VUWTC Tararua Tramp Week involves 2 groups tramping the Southern Crossing over the weekend, coming out on Sunday. The reason for having 2 groups is so that we can do a car swap, exchanging keys when we meet along the track and driving theother groups vehicle(s) home. One group will tramp from Kaitoke to Otaki Forks and the other from Otaki Forks to Kaitoke. Below plans are outlined for each group.

Southern Crossing

DRIVERS NEEDED (Jack will give you a piece of his Blue Boots as a reward <3 )

 

Kaitoke - otaki Forks Group

Day 1 Friday 16th: 

Meet at Hunter Carpark at 7:30am, depart at 8am. Arrive at Kaitoke Roadend and on track by 9am. Tramp to Alpha Hut via Marchant Ridge. (8-10h)

 

Day 2 Saturday 17th:

Tramp from Alpha Hut to Kime Hut, meeting other group on the way, swapping car keys. (5-6h)

 

Day 3 Sunday 18th:

Walk out from Kime Hut to Otaki Forks, drive back to Hunter Carpark, swap back cars. Make ways home! (6-8h)

 

 

Otaki Forks - Kaitoke Group

Day 1 Friday 16th:

Meet at Hunter Carpark at 4:30pm, depart by 5pm. Arrive at Otaki Forks and on track by 6:15pm. Tramp to Field Hut !!Headtorches!! (3-4h)

 

Day 2 Saturday 17th: 

Tramp from Field Hut to Alpha Hut, meeting other group on the way, swapping car keys. (8-10h)

 

Day 3 Sunday 18th: 

Walk out from Alpha Hut to Kaitoke Road End via Marchant Ridge, drive back to Hunter Carpark, swap back cars. Make ways home! (8-9h)

**All times are according to DOC**

 

**Alternative Group Option** 

We are looking at the option of one group doing this route as a trail run over one day from Kaitoke to Otaki Forks on the Sunday. (35km)

 

Hut Tickets

Each Hut on this trip requires 1 standard hut ticket. This means that everyone should be bringing 2 Blue Hut Tickets.

Hut Ticket Retailers

 

Gear

Please bring overnight tramping gear. Key things are a tramping pack, warm clothing, a windproof raincoat, tramping boots or trail runners, a sleeping bag, a bowl/cutlery, a headtorch, a 1-2L water, and a first aid kit/personal medication. Bring a pot/cooker if you have them.

You can borrow club gear (eg packs, sleeping bags), but please ask in advance.

VUWTC Gear List

 

Fitness Levels

Due to some longish days on track and long exposed sections along the tops, this trip will require a moderate level of tramping experience.

 

Food

You will need to organise your own food for this trip. 

You will need to bring:

  • Dinner x 2
  • Lunch (x3 for Kaitoke to Otaki Forks group, x2 for Otaki Forks to Kaitoke Group)
  • Breakfast x 2
  • Snacks!!

Food inspo

Huts do not have gas provided so if you have a cooker you are able to bring along to share with the group please let me know.

Note: Feel free to organise shared meals with others in your group if you wish.

Te Matawai mish (Tramps Week) (Thursday, 15 - Friday, 16 October 2026)Click to expand

Organiser:
Hannah Ellis-Ducobu

 

TE MATAWAI MISSION!!!

Unfortunately I have exams so can't afford any longer tramps :( but never fear! The humble overnighter offers a welcome escape from the drudgery of study period. I want to go to Te Matawai hut because I've never explored that area of the Tararuas and theres's no better time than now. This is a medium level tramp so some experience and fitness is required - if you're unsure flick me an email :)

 

DATE FLEXIBLE: if you're keen but can't make these dates let me know and I could potentially change to a nearby day.

 

^This could be your home for an evening...

^ and this could be you making an epic journey up Pukematawai peak...

PLAN:

Day 1:
Meet at Hunter carpark around 7:30am, and drive 1h15m to Poads Rd end. Walk up to Te Matawai hut (around 900m total elevation gain), via Waiopehu hut and Butcher Saddle. The Doc estimate is 6 hours, but it could be longer depending on group size and experience. We should get to the hut before dark.

Day 2:

I'm thinking a morning mission up to Pukematawai peak for those who are keen?? Then back more or less the way we came (there are two options of similar distance so we can decide) and back to Welly. All going well we should be back by early evening.

 

WHAT TO BRING:
- Standard overnight tramping gear (https://www.vuwtc.org.nz/resources/gear-list). Don't forget warm layers and a headtorch + spare batteries + toilet paper ;)

- Water, lunch x2, breakfast, and snacks. I'll sort a group dinner and distribute ingredients before we leave.

 

If someone else with a car signs up more people can come!
